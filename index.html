04 // How I work

Engineering principles, not vibes.

PROTOCOL 01 — Production first

Demos are easy. Systems are the job.

Anything I build assumes real traffic, real failure modes, and real users — auth, retries, observability, and cost controls from day one.

PROTOCOL 02 — Measure everything

If it isn't evaluated, it isn't finished.

Retrieval quality, agent behavior, latency, and cost are all measurable. I make decisions from evals and traces, not intuition.

PROTOCOL 03 — Architecture over hype

The model is 20% of the system.

The other 80% is data quality, retrieval design, orchestration, and infrastructure. That's where reliable AI products are actually won.

PROTOCOL 04 — Simple scales

Complexity must earn its place.

I reach for managed services and boring, proven patterns first — and add sophistication only where it measurably improves outcomes.
